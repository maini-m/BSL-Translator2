Hold the ✕ for half a second. Your shoulders and wrists need to be in view.

✨ Better grammar with AI (optional)

Without this, built-in rules tidy the sentence (works offline). With a Google Gemini API key, AI rewrites it into natural English and suggests alternatives.

Only the recognised words are sent to Google, never video. The key stays in this browser; never put it in GitHub. Google's terms require API users to be 18+, so ask a teacher or mentor to create the key at aistudio.google.com.

Hold each letter steady until it appears. Lower your hands for a moment to finish a word, or before repeating the same letter.

Raise it if wrong words keep appearing. Lower it if correct signs are being missed.

Record a sign

After a 3-second countdown, sign once (about 1.5 seconds). Press Space to record. Aim for 15+ samples per word from different people.

Also record a word called rest: hands visible but not signing. It's never added to the translation, and it stops idle hands being read as a sign.

    Teach it your hands

    The built-in model learned from someone else's hands. Record a few examples of letters it gets wrong, and it will use them. This also adds C, H, J and Y, which the dataset doesn't include.

    Aim for 3 teaching recordings per problem letter; move your hands slightly between them. For H and J, hold the end position.
